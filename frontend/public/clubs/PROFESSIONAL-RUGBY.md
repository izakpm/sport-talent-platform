# Professional rugby directory assets

Added 23 November 2026. Exact source URLs are recorded in `frontend/app/network/clubs.json` as `logoSource`, with official website links in `websites.json`.

- Blue Bulls: official Blue Bulls Rugby Union website; reuses `/organisations/blue-bulls.png`.
- Stormers: official Stormers team asset.
- Sharks and Lions: opponent logo assets published by the official Stormers website.
- Cheetahs, Pumas and Griquas: each organisation's official website.

Original assets are unchanged. The white Stormers and Sharks logos use a dark CSS background for contrast. Logos belong to their respective rights holders. These entries are directory listings, not established partnerships; network logos retain the existing greyscale treatment. Achievement logos identify the selected organisation and do not verify achievements.
